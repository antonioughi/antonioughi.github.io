Ciao!

I am a Ph.D. student at the Paris School of Economics (PSE) and an affiliate of the Farhi Innovation Lab at the Collège de France.

My research studies how technology diffuses across firms through innovation and production networks. I am particularly interested in how private finance and industrial policy shape technological diffusion and spillovers.

Before joining PSE, I worked as an Economic and Policy Analyst at the OECD in the Directorate for Science, Technology and Innovation. I also held visiting research positions at the Bank of Italy, the Italian Ministry of Economic Development, the Italian Ministry of Economy and Finance, and the Italian National Institute of Statistics. I hold a master’s degree in Economics from PSE and an honours master's degree in Economics and Management from the Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies.
