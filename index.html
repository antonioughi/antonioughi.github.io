Ciao!

I am a Ph.D. student at the Paris School of Economics (PSE) and Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, and an affiliate of the Innovation Lab at the Collège de France.

My research focuses on how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and other advanced automation technologies spread across firms and affect productivity growth. I am particularly interested in quantifying the role of industrial and technology policies in shaping technology diffusion and influencing business dynamism.

Before joining PSE, I worked as a Policy Analyst at the OECD in the Directorate for Science, Technology and Innovation. I hold master’s degrees in Economics from PSE and the Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies.
