Research CV

Ciao!

I am a Ph.D. student at the Paris School of Economics (PSE) and Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, and an affiliate of the Innovation Lab at the Collège de France.

My research explores the diffusion patterns of AI and other automation technologies across firms, with a focus on their implications for business dynamism and productivity. I am also interested in how industrial and technology policies influence both the diffusion of these technologies and their economic returns.

Before joining PSE, I worked as a Policy Analyst at the OECD in the Directorate for Science, Technology and Innovation. I hold a master’s degree in Economics from the Paris School of Economics and the Sant’Anna School of Advanced Studies.

This August 2025 I am attending a summer course at Stanford University on the Economics of AI.
